## Policy & Asset Rules

1. **No Proprietary Brand Assets**: No assets were copied or extracted from Hyundai, third-party dealership brochures, or proprietary manufacturer catalogs.
2. **Provisional Equipment Illustrations**: Until Nanjappa confirms specific inventory models and provides genuine photography of machines in Hosur, original, purpose-crafted SVG technical illustrations are used. This avoids deceptive marketing and ensures compliance with advertising guidelines.
3. **Owner Checklist Link**: Real owner-provided photos can replace these SVG files directly inside `public/images/` without altering page structure.
