# Page and catalog notes

## Site structure and visible interactions

The forklift landing page is part of a larger construction-equipment site. Its global navigation includes product search, dealer lookup, product families, parts, service, company information, and contact links. The forklift family shown in the navigation is Electric, divided into a 3-wheel family and a 4-wheel family. The forklift category page itself has an overview, industry images, link to electric forklifts, FAQ entries, a video area, and a dealer lookup that asks for a six-digit PIN code.

The electric category page has an introductory paragraph, four product-family cards with a few summary specs, brochure links, and quote calls to action. Its FAQ follows. Each individual product page is structured with Overview / Specifications / Features navigation, a specification table, feature groups, product imagery, a brochure/specification download, quote action, and dealer search. Feature group themes are performance, operator productivity/comfort, safety, and maintenance.

The pages expose quote/enquiry UI with fields including name, phone, email, mobile, location, organization, and selects. The category page has a separate dealer PIN search. This research establishes what is visible on the pages, not whether each form was submitted or how each backend behaves.

For a distinct small-business landing page, this information architecture can be simplified to: original hero, confirmed product range, owner-confirmed services, a few FAQs, a simple contact route, and clear local contact details. A direct WhatsApp click-to-chat action can open the visitor's messaging app; true in-page live chat or staff notifications need an explicitly chosen messaging service and recipient setup.

## FAQ and contact themes

The generic forklift FAQ covers uses, forklift types, how a forklift works, selection factors, after-sales/warranty, and safety. The electric category FAQ covers battery power, comparisons with fuel-powered trucks, capacity, operating costs, and outdoor use. Individual model FAQ pages add model-specific questions.

There are inconsistencies in the page copy. For example, the BTR-9 model table gives the range as 1,000-1,500 kg while an FAQ answer says "15 tons"; the electric-category FAQ also uses a general capacity range that does not match the published model table. Use the tables and currently approved manufacturer brochures as research references, and verify with the owner/manufacturer instead of copying FAQ claims.
